About

Who is
this guy?

Backend engineer by profession, existential crisis manager by default. I write code that talks to other code through APIs because apparently that's a career now. Currently stationed at Cognizant in Kolkata, doing the kind of work that sounds impressive at parties but is impossible to explain to relatives.

"He could've become a doctor or an engineer." — I chose both, in a way. I engineer things that give me a headache.

When I'm not wrangling microservices or watching Kubernetes pods crash for sport, I travel to places that have no internet — which is the only real vacation from the job. I also co-founded an AI startup, because apparently debugging production issues at work wasn't enough chaos in my life.
